Sudokuoku

## Short description

Sudoku that shifts after each move and stays solvable.

## Description

Solve Sudoku while the board shifts around you. Rows and columns slide, the board turns and mirrors, and each transformation preserves the puzzle and your entries.

Use pencil notes, hints and Undo. Try the daily board, phantom challenges and six visual packs with light and dark modes. Share a challenge code with a friend, or send a completed run so they can race your ghost. High contrast and reduce motion settings are available.

## Data notes for the privacy-policy author

Game progress and settings are stored locally. Game/challenge codes deliberately include information needed to replay a shared game; players choose who receives them. These notes do not replace a published privacy policy or a review of platform, hosting and future billing data practices.

The website, and only the website, can offer sign-in with Google and a one-time "Support this app" payment through Stripe Checkout, where its host runs the sign-in API and the owner has set its keys (README, "Sign-in and Support this app"; Google's consent screen asks for this policy's URL). Nothing leaves the visitor's device for it unless they sign in or buy. Signing in, Google tells the site an account id and the email address; a payment goes through Stripe, which handles it (the card never reaches the site). The site keeps the account id, what was bought and Stripe's customer id, and the email address only in the sign-in session (gone at sign-out, or after thirty days). Delete account removes them, but for a count of the hour's Buy presses that names the account id and expires within two hours; the payment itself stays with Stripe. The iOS and Android apps have none of this: no account, no payment and no network code.
